Missing faces tool

Each row is one shot. Play the shot and check that every human, Muppet and animal face has a white box. If they all do, press Enter ("All faces boxed", then the next shot). If a face has no box, press f ("Face missing"), stop on a frame where that face is clearly visible, drag a box around it on the video, then press c (child), d (adult), m (Muppet) or a (animal; also type which animal). Add one box for each missing face. Your work saves in this browser automatically, but click Export CSV when you stop for the day.

White boxes = faces SAM3 found. Yellow boxes = missing faces you marked (shown on the frame you drew them).
