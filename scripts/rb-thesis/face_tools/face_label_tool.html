Face label tool

Each row in Faces in this clip is one face that SAM3 tracked through one shot. SAM3's prompt already says whether most faces are human, Muppet or animal, and that answer is pre-selected. Check the blue box: if the answer is right, press Enter; if not, pick the right one (or Not a face if the box is wrong), then Enter. Some faces came from a generic prompt and have no answer yet, so you pick one. (Faces SAM3 missed entirely are checked in a separate tool, missing_faces_tool.html.) Your work saves in this browser automatically, but click Export CSV when you stop for the day.

Blue box = the face you are labeling. White boxes = other faces in the same frame (with their label, if they have one).
