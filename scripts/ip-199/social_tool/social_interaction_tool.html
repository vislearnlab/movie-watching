Social interaction tool

Each row is one segment (the stretch between two cuts). Play the segment (space), then answer the four questions. People means humans and Muppets (not animals). Count everyone who appears at any point in the segment. The videos have no sound: judge talking from mouth movement. Press Enter to save and go to the next segment. Your work saves in this browser automatically, but click Export CSV when you stop for the day.

1. How many people are in this segment?

Humans + Muppets, everyone who appears at any point. Type a number, or press a number key 0–9.

2. Is someone talking to the viewer?

A person looks at the camera and their mouth moves as if speaking to you.

3. Communicative interaction?

Two or more people talking with each other (mouths moving, taking turns).

4. Physical interaction?

Two or more people doing something together without talking: touching, playing, dancing, passing things. Can be Yes along with question 3.
